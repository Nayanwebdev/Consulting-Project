# Consulting Web

A professional and modern **business consulting website** designed to present consulting services, showcase case studies, highlight team members, and build trust through testimonials, statistics, and company content.

## 🌐 Live Website

[Consulting Web](https://consulting-web.netlify.app/)

## ✨ Features

* Professional consulting-focused homepage
* Strategy Planning service section
* Insurance Service section
* Audit & Evaluation section
* About Us section
* Featured Case Studies
* Client testimonial section
* Project and client statistics
* Professional team members section
* Consultant call-to-action section
* Recent Blog / News section
* Responsive corporate website layout
* Clean and business-oriented visual presentation

## 📌 Main Sections

### Services

The website presents multiple consulting services, including:

* Strategy Planning
* Insurance Service
* Audit & Evaluation

### Featured Case Studies

A portfolio-style section showcases featured consulting cases and projects.

### Testimonials

Client-focused testimonial content is presented to build credibility and trust.

### Company Statistics

The homepage includes business statistics such as:

* Project Completed
* Active Client
* Cups of Coffee
* Happy Clients

### Our Team

A dedicated team section introduces professional members and their roles, including UX Designer profiles.

### Blog

The website also contains a recent news/blog section for publishing company-related articles and updates.

## 🎯 Project Goals

The main goal of this project is to create a professional online presence for a consulting business while clearly communicating its services, experience, team, and previous work.

## 🎨 Design

* Corporate and professional visual style
* Service-focused content hierarchy
* Large image-based sections
* Clear call-to-action areas
* Case-study presentation
* Team and testimonial layouts
* Responsive design approach
